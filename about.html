About StayNearby

StayNearby is a web-based platform that helps college students find affordable PG accommodations near their campus, while allowing PG owners to list their properties.

Why I built this

Students moving to a new city for college often struggle to find a PG near their campus at a price they can afford. Listings are scattered across word of mouth, local brokers, and random social media posts, with no single place to compare options. StayNearby brings students and PG owners onto one platform to make this search simpler.

Who it's for

Students looking for a nearby, budget-friendly place to stay, and PG owners who want their property seen by students in the area.

How it works

  1. Search for PGs near your college
  2. Filter by price, room type, and amenities
  3. Contact the owner directly to finalize your stay

Project info
