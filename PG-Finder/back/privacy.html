Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

StayNearby is a college project. This demo version does not actually store or send any information you type into its forms.

1. What we collect

  • Your name, email, and phone number when you sign up.
  • PG details when an owner lists a property, such as name, address, price, and photos.
  • Feedback and reviews you choose to submit.

2. How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show PG listings, and let students and owners contact each other.

3. What is shared

An owner's name and contact details are shown on their listing so students can reach them. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies

A real version of StayNearby may use cookies to keep you logged in and remember your filters.

5. Keeping your data safe

We aim to protect your information, but no website can promise complete security. Please choose a strong password and keep it private.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your account and listings at any time.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@staynearby.com.
