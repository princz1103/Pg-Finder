Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions from students and PG owners.

For students

How do I find a PG near my college?

Go to the Find a PG page, type your college or area in the search box, and use the filters to narrow down by price, distance, room type, and services.

How do I contact a PG owner?

Open a PG's details page to see the owner's name, phone, and email. Some listings also have a "Call owner" button directly on the card.

Is there any charge for students?

No. Searching, comparing, and contacting owners is completely free for students.

Can I compare different PGs?

Yes. The Compare page shows PGs side by side with their price, distance, room type, and services, so you can pick the best fit.

For PG owners

How do I list my PG?

Click List your PG in the menu, fill in the form with your PG's name, address, price, room type, and services, add a photo, and submit.

Do I need an account to list my PG?

Yes. Create a free account from the Sign up page and log in before listing your PG.

Can I edit or remove my listing later?

Yes. Owners can update or remove their listing at any time from their dashboard.

About the site

Are the PGs on StayNearby verified?

PGs marked with a Verified badge have been checked by our team. We recommend visiting a PG in person before paying any deposit.

I have another question. Who can I ask?

Use the Help button at the bottom-right of any page, or email us at support@staynearby.com.
