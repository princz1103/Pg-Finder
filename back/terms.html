By using StayNearby, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. Using StayNearby

StayNearby helps students find PG accommodations near their college and helps PG owners reach students. We are a listing platform only. We do not own, manage, or run any of the PGs shown.

3. For students

  • Visit a PG in person before paying any deposit or rent.
  • Check the room, rules, and services directly with the owner.
  • Use the contact details only to ask about accommodation.

4. For PG owners

  • Give accurate details, prices, and real photos of your PG.
  • Keep your listing up to date, and remove it when rooms are full.
  • Treat students fairly and honestly.

5. Payments and deposits

StayNearby does not take part in any payment between students and owners. Any deposit, rent, or agreement is only between the two of them.
